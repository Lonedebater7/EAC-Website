About EAC

Practical industrial controls engineering, built from field experience.

Elwell Automation Consulting LLC is a Washington-based industrial controls and automation engineering company founded by Brandon Elwell.

EAC was built around a practical engineering approach: understand how automated equipment actually behaves in production, identify the technical cause of problems, and carry solutions through engineering, field validation and commissioning.

Brandon's background spans PLC/SCADA engineering, industrial troubleshooting, automated material handling, commissioning, reliability, system integration, technical leadership and training. As EAC expands its engineering team, engineers are technically screened and supported through EAC.
